Viruses

When people say virus they most likely mean biological viruses rather than computer viruses.


Biological viruses:

contrary to popular belief, viruses are actually not living things and are instead basically DNA
capsules that invade your cells and make more of themselves using the cell they infect and their own DNA.

list of viruses:

  • Rhinovirus (common cold)
  • Coronavirus
  • Norovirus

viruses like these are all technically catagories containing multiple different strands,
varing in danger.


Computer viruses:

computer viruses on the other hand work pretty much the same way but instead of cells they infect files
or folders. They do not duplicate, most of the time. Also contrary to popular belief; not all malware are viruses.
